Ilmizo Privacy Policy

App Package: com.ilmizo.tarbiyah · Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Ilmizo — Islamic Learning & Family Tarbiyah is built around a strict local-first privacy commitment for adults, parents, and children. We do not require accounts, do not serve third-party advertisements, and do not operate analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Learning & Family Records: Quran reading progress, Hifz revision schedules, Salah logs, journal notes, kids profiles, stars, badges, and parent screen-time rules are stored locally on your device in IndexedDB and LocalStorage.
  • Parent PIN & Recovery Security: Parent PINs and recovery codes are hashed on-device using PBKDF2-SHA256 via the browser/WebView Web Crypto API. Plaintext PINs are never stored or transmitted.
  • Backups & Sync Bundles: JSON and encrypted .ilmizo-sync family files are exported and imported only when initiated manually by you.

2. Permissions Used & Why

  • Notifications & Exact Alarms (Optional): Used strictly to deliver local on-device Azan and Salah reminders configured by you.
  • Microphone (Optional — Kids Practice Only): When a child taps the practice microphone button, live audio amplitude is analyzed locally in memory for visual voice feedback. Audio is never recorded, saved to disk, or uploaded to any server.
  • Location (Optional): Used only when you choose to fetch local prayer timings or calculate Qibla direction. Coordinates are never stored on external Ilmizo servers.
  • Internet Access (Optional): Used only when you explicitly request online Qari audio recitations, Word-by-Word Quran dictionary downloads, or prayer timing lookups from public Islamic APIs (such as AlQuran Cloud / Quran.com / AlAdhan).

3. Children’s Privacy & Google Play Families Compliance

Ilmizo’s Kids World is designed for safe family tarbiyah without ads, social feeds, in-app purchases, external tracking links, or personal data collection from children. All child profiles remain strictly on the family’s device under Parent Hub PIN control.

4. Deleting Your Data

You can delete or reset all local Ilmizo data at any time inside the app via More > Local Data & Backup or by uninstalling the application from your device.

5. Contact

For privacy questions regarding com.ilmizo.tarbiyah, contact the developer listed on the Ilmizo Google Play Store page.
